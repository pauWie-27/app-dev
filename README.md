# My Favorite Series -

**Title:** Alice in Borderland  
**Genre:** Sci-Fi, Thriller, Mystery, Drama  

**Why I love it:**  
I love the intense story of survival, friendship, and human nature as Arisu and his friends face deadly games in a deserted version of Tokyo known as the **Borderland**.  

> "In this world, you play... or you die."

### Main Characters
- **Ryohei Arisu** - A smart and logical gamer who becomes a natural leader in the Borderland.  
- **Yuzuha Usagi** - A brave and athletic mountain climber who values survival and trust.  
- **Shuntarō Chishiya** - A calm and intelligent strategist who prefers to observe and plan.  
- **Hikari Kuina** - A skilled fighter with a strong sense of loyalty and self-acceptance.
